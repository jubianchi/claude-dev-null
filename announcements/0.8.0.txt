https://github.com/jubianchi/claude-dev-null 0.8.0 is out, and the log pane does a lot more than read.

You can now act on what you read without leaving Claude Code: vote in polls, star commits with ⭐️, open the whole thread of a commit with 🧵, and unfold long commits right in the pane.

The pane got a Starred tab, and a ⚙ for your account, auto refresh, how many replies to show (as on the site) and where the composer lives: keep it above the prompt while you read the log, or let it move into the pane.

Auto refresh no longer throws you back to the top: new commits wait above, behind a "↑ N new commits" badge, as on the site.

Also: threads read better, their lanes joined to their replies, one level deeper.
