https://github.com/jubianchi/claude-dev-null 0.7.0 is out, and the browser cookie is gone for good.

The plugin is now a device of yours: approve it once from the Account tab, and it reads your logs from the /dev/null API with a key of its own. The cookie earlier versions kept is wiped on the next start. If you used it, sign in again from Account to get your Following log back.

Until terminals get their own sign-in (https://github.com/dev-null-sh/dev-null.sh/pull/585), the approval goes the way the mobile app's does: approve on dev-null.sh, then paste the link it sends back. A bit clunky, but no more secret cookie lying around.

Also in this release: commits of a single character, links counted as /dev/null counts them, quotes and deleted commits kept in their threads, GitHub links shown as owner/repo#123, and long commits folded like on the site.
